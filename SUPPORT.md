# Getting help

- **Bug or render mismatch:** Open an issue with the matching template, reproduction steps, and minimal input files.
- **Usage question:** Check the [README](README.md) and [docs/](docs/README.md) first. If anything is still unclear, open an issue with the **Question** label.
- **Security vulnerability:** Report it privately via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue.

## Out of scope

This tracker is strictly for `roblox-ui-renderer`. Issues about the following will be closed:
- Roblox account issues, purchases, bans, or specific Roblox games.
- Requests to download assets, places, or models you do not own, or to bypass Roblox moderation or permissions.
- Cheats, exploits, or injectors.

Maintainers work on this in their own time, so replies may take a few days.
